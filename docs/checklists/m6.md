# M6 listening checklist — chip instruments

Sign-off by the owner (PLAN.md §9, M6 acceptance). Tick each item after listening; note anything that
sounds wrong in the last column. Use headphones or the monitor speakers, master around -12 dB.

Setup: `LOAD <path to>/tests/fixtures/chip.phosphor`, then F9 (Page C). Instruments: 1 LEAD (GB pulse),
2 BASS (GB wave), 3 NOISE (GB noise), 4 PAD (SNES, from `loop_forward.wav`). `INST <n>` selects one; play
it from the KeyStep (or KEYJAZZ, MOD+K). Space plays the demo pattern on Page R.

Automated facts this checklist does not repeat: pulse/wave pitch = period formulas, LFSR periods
32767/127, BRR decoding per the fullsnes equations, echo FIR impulse response, STRICT voice limits
(`tests/unit/test_chip.cpp`). BRR encode->decode SNR on a 440 Hz sine: **54.5 dB** (baseline, 2026-11-26).
