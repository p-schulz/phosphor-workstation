# M7 listening checklist — vaporwave toolkit

Sign-off by the owner (PLAN.md §9, M7). Tick each item after listening; note anything that sounds
wrong in the last column. Master around -12 dB; headphones or monitors.

Setup: `NEW MALLSOFT` (or `NEW FUTURE-FUNK`, `NEW CHIP-VAPOR`), F10 (Page L), Space plays. Page M edits
inserts, sends and returns (ENTER on an effect opens its parameters).

Automated facts this checklist does not repeat: tempo reflow in all three sync modes, STRETCH cache
time (0.15 s for 8 bars), launch quantize timing, LOOPS lanes, FX formulas, allocation-free
rendering (`tests/unit/test_vapor.cpp`).
